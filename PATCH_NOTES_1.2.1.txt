PowerPointLite 1.2.1
====================

Fix
---
1.2 introduced two WinForms timers while System.Threading was also imported.
The unqualified Timer type caused CS0104 on .NET Framework csc.exe.

Fixed by using System.Windows.Forms.Timer explicitly for:
- sidebarHideTimer
- PresenterViewForm timer

No .NET 8 SDK is required.
